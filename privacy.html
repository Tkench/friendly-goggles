Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy explains what information Last Brick collects, why, and what you can do about it. We've tried to keep it short and in plain English.

Who we are

Last Brick (lastbrick.co.uk) is an app for tracking missing pieces in LEGO® sets. Last Brick is responsible for your personal information (the "data controller"). You can contact us at hello@lastbrick.co.uk.

Using the app without an account

You can try Last Brick without creating an account, with one set or one minifigure (to add more, you'll need a free account). Without an account, your set or minifigure, ticks and values are saved only on your device. We never receive them.

What we collect if you create an account

  • Your email address – to log you in and to send account emails (confirming your email, resetting your password).
  • Your password – stored only in scrambled (hashed) form. Nobody, including us, can read it.
  • Your Last Brick data – your sets, which pieces you've ticked, set values you've entered, records of sets you've deleted (so deletions sync between devices) and, only if you've chosen to add one, your own Rebrickable API key.
  • Technical information – our hosting provider automatically records things like IP address, device or browser type and times of access, to keep the service secure and working.

We don't collect your name, address, phone number, location or payment details. We don't use advertising or tracking tools, and we never sell your information.

Counting visits

To know roughly how many people use Last Brick, the app records a visit once a day per device. A visit is only the date, three yes/no answers (is this the first time on this device, is someone logged in, and is the app installed on the home screen) and where the visit came from: the name of the website that linked to us (such as facebook.com) or a tag in the link (such as ?ref=reddit). It contains no IP address, account, device ID or anything else that identifies you, and uses no cookies. (To avoid counting you twice in a day, the app remembers on your device that it has already counted today.) We use these totals only to see how the app is doing (our legitimate interests).

If you report a problem

If you use Report a problem or Wrong match? Tell us, we receive what you write, plus what the app knew at the time: what the scanner guessed (names, part or figure numbers and how sure it was), what the app showed you and which page you were on. We also receive:

  • your email address, only if you type it in so we can reply;
  • a small copy of your scan photo, only if you tick "include my photo";
  • a link to your account, if you're logged in.

We use reports only to fix problems and improve the app (our legitimate interests), and to reply if you asked us to.

Why we use it (our lawful basis)

  • To provide the account, backup and sync you asked for – this is necessary to provide the service (contract).
  • To keep the service secure and prevent misuse – our legitimate interests.
  • To reply if you contact us.

Other services the app uses

Some features connect directly from your device to other services. They receive only what they need:

  • Supabase stores account data and handles logins for us, and runs our parts-list lookup. Data is stored in their London (UK) data centre.
  • Our parts-list lookup receives the set numbers you add. It fetches the parts list from Rebrickable using Last Brick's own key and keeps a shared copy, so the next person adding that set gets it straight away. The shared copy isn't linked to you.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the app's files, and like any website may log your IP address when the app loads.
  • Rebrickable supplies the parts lists (through our lookup) and the piece pictures, which load straight from Rebrickable to your device. If you've added your own Rebrickable key, the app may use it to contact Rebrickable directly when our lookup is unavailable. Rebrickable's privacy policy applies.
  • Brickognize receives the photos you take with Scan, to identify the piece. Photos go straight from your device to Brickognize; we don't receive or keep them. Please only photograph LEGO pieces. Brickognize's terms apply.
  • BrickLink – if you tap a "See prices" link, BrickLink's website opens in your browser and its own privacy policy applies.

Some of these companies are based in the United States and may process information there. Where that happens, it is protected by the safeguards UK law requires, such as approved contract terms.

How long we keep it

We keep problem reports for up to 12 months, then delete them. We keep your account and data until you delete your account. When you do, they are removed from our database straight away; copies in our provider's routine backups are overwritten within a short period. Technical logs are kept by our providers for a limited time for security.

Children

You must be 13 or over to create an account. Anyone can use the app without an account, with everything kept on their device. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 13, we'll delete it. Parents can contact us at hello@lastbrick.co.uk.

Storage on your device

The app saves your sets, settings and login on your device so it works offline and keeps you logged in. This is essential for the app to work, so we don't ask for cookie consent. We don't use cookies or storage for tracking or advertising.

Keeping it safe

Information is encrypted while travelling between your device and our services. Each account can only read and change its own data, and passwords are hashed. No system is perfectly secure, but if a breach ever affected your information we would tell you and the regulator as the law requires.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • see the personal information we hold about you;
  • correct it if it's wrong;
  • delete it – you can do this yourself in Settings → Delete my account;
  • take a copy – Settings → Download backup gives you your data as a file;
  • object to or restrict how we use it.

To use any of these rights, email hello@lastbrick.co.uk. We'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

If we make important changes we'll update the date at the top and, where appropriate, tell you in the app.
